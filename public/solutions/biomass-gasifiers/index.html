Biomass gasifiers and CHP systems

Turn wood into a gas that fires your boiler, kiln or dryer, or runs a generator. Designed and fabricated by EnerFab in Sri Lanka.

What we replace

Off diesel, furnace oil and LPG

EnerFab biomass systems can replace diesel, furnace oil (HFO) and LPG fired boilers, furnaces, kilns and dryers, and the fuel in internal combustion engines that drive electrical generators.

We can also install combined heat and power (CHP) plants that supply electricity while using the waste heat for thermal energy. EnerFab is known for proposing new ways to cut the cost of thermal and electrical systems, using gasifiers we design and fabricate ourselves.

Because we run energy plantations and an out-grower network of biomass suppliers, we can also supply your biomass on contract, so the plant always has fuel.

  • Combined heat and power (CHP) systems

How the conversion works

  1. Fuelwood in

    Fuelwood such as Gliricidia is chopped into small pieces and fed to the gasifier.

  2. Gasification

    The gasifier converts the solid fuel into a combustible producer gas.

  3. Piped like LPG

    The gas is piped to a burner on your boiler or dryer, or to a gas engine.

  4. Heat and power

    Thermal energy, electricity, or both together as combined heat and power.

Thermal: replacing 50 L/hr of diesel

Thermal applications

Gasification converts solid fuel into a combustible gas, which can be piped just like LPG to a burner on a boiler or dryer.

This dryer in a rubber processing plant had a diesel burner. The EnerFab team replaced it with a producer-gas burner and a gasifier, and it has operated from 2006 to date.

Electrical: gasifier generator set

Electrical applications

Producer gas can run an internal combustion engine, which turns a generator to produce electricity.

EnerFab installed this gasifier generator at a wood processing plant in Sri Lanka. It makes 200 kW of electricity from waste wood, and has an unused potential of 400 kW of thermal energy from the engine’s exhaust and cooling system.

Serving industry since 2005

Kandalama Hotel and Tea Factory Hotel of the Aitken Spence Group, CW Mackie and other establishments have operated EnerFab gasifiers for more than a decade. In 2017 EnerFab installed a 200 kW electrical system, showing producer gas is clean enough to run an internal combustion engine.

We have provided after-sales service to these industries since 2005. These long-running installations have proven economical while being far kinder to the environment.
