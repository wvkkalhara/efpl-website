Furnace oil to biomass: converting an industrial boiler

How Sri Lankan factories switch diesel and furnace-oil boilers to biomass with a gasifier and dual-fuel burner: the two routes, the steps, and what data to gather first.

EnerFab engineering
14 November 2026
3 min read

Key points

  • There are two ways to move a boiler to biomass: replace it with a wood-fired boiler, or keep it and fire it with producer gas from a gasifier.
  • With a gasifier and dual-fuel burner you keep your existing boiler, and your old fuel stays connected as a backup.
  • Good projects start with data: how much fuel you burn, for how many hours, and how your heat demand varies.
  • Securing the fuelwood supply matters as much as the equipment.

Why factories move off oil

Furnace oil (heavy fuel oil, HFO), diesel and LPG are imported fuels, so their cost follows world oil prices and the exchange rate. Biomass such as fuelwood is grown locally, is renewable when it comes from managed plantations, and keeps fuel spending inside the rural economy. For factories that need heat every day, such as tea, rubber, food processing and hotels, that is a strong reason to look at biomass.

Step by step

  1. Measure what you use today. Litres of oil or diesel per hour and per month, operating hours and days, steam or heat demand, and how the load rises and falls through the day.
  2. Fix the losses first. An energy audit often finds savings in steam traps, insulation, condensate return and combustion settings. There is no point sizing a gasifier for heat that is being wasted.
  3. Size the gasifier to the real heat demand, including peaks.
  4. Plan the fuel. Decide where the fuelwood comes from, how it will be delivered, stored, dried and chopped, and who manages supply through the year.
  5. Lay out the site. Room for wood storage, a chopper, the gasifier, the gas line to the boiler house, and access for deliveries.
  6. Install and connect. Gasifier, gas piping, dual-fuel burner and controls, so the boiler can switch between producer gas and its original fuel.
  7. Commission and train. Tune the system and train operators in daily running, fuel loading and ash removal.
  8. Maintain. Plan regular maintenance and spare parts, or hand operation to a contractor.

What to send when you ask for a quote

  • Boiler or heater type, make, rated capacity and current fuel
  • Fuel use: litres per hour at typical load, and per month
  • Operating hours per day and days per week
  • Steam pressure or process temperature needed
  • Space available near the boiler house
  • Any biomass you already have on site, such as off-cuts or husks

EnerFab conversions in practice

EnerFab replaced the diesel burner on a rubber processing plant’s dryer, which used 50 litres of diesel an hour, with a producer-gas burner and gasifier; it has run from 2006 to date. Kandalama Hotel, Tea Factory Hotel, CW Mackie and other establishments have operated EnerFab gasifiers for more than a decade. EnerFab can also supply the fuelwood on contract, and offers management contracts, spare parts and breakdown maintenance.
