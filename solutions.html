01 · Biomass applications

Biomass Gasifiers

Biomass systems supplied by EnerFab can replace diesel, furnace oil (HFO) and LPG in boilers, furnaces, kilns and dryers, and in the internal combustion engines of electrical generators.

EnerFab can install combined heat and power plants that supply electricity while using the waste heat to supply thermal energy.

EnerFab is well known for proposing innovative solutions that cut the cost of thermal and electrical systems, using gasifiers designed and fabricated at EnerFab.

EnerFab undertakes the supply of biomass for customers on a contractual basis. EnerFab has energy plantations and an out-grower network of biomass suppliers, ensuring a consistent supply of fuelwood.

EnerFab offers

  • Biomass gasifiers for industrial heating and electrical power generation
  • Supply of fuelwood from the short-rotation coppicing of fast-growing leguminous trees
  • Management contracts to operate plants supplied by EnerFab
  • Spare parts and breakdown maintenance for all installations completed
Thermal: replacing 50 L/hr of diesel

Thermal applications

The gasifier can be used to switch from diesel, furnace oil and LPG to biomass. Gasification converts solid fuel into a combustible gas. This gas can be piped, just like LPG, to a burner fitted to a boiler or dryer.

The video shows a dryer in a rubber processing plant that had a diesel burner using 50 litres of diesel per hour. The EnerFab team replaced it with a producer-gas burner and a gasifier. This unit has been in operation from 2006 to date.

Electrical: gasifier generator set

Electrical applications

The producer gas from a gasifier can be used to run an internal combustion engine. In the video, the engine turns a generator to produce electricity.

EnerFab installed this gasifier generator in a wood-processing industry in Sri Lanka. It produces 200 kW of electricity from the waste wood of the manufacturing process. The unit also has an unused potential of 400 kW of thermal energy that can be harvested from the engine’s exhaust and cooling system.

Continuously serving the industry since 2005

EnerFab-installed gasifiers have been in operation for over a decade in several industries in Sri Lanka. Kandalama Hotel and Tea Factory Hotel of the Aitken Spence Group, CW Mackie and a few more establishments have been operating EnerFab gasifiers for over a decade.

In 2017 EnerFab installed a 200 kW electrical system, proving that producer gas is clean enough to run an internal combustion engine. In July 2018 EnerFab undertook to install a gasification plant to replace LPG at a poultry farm in Puttalam, Sri Lanka. This project is still ongoing.

EnerFab has continuously provided after-sales services for these industries since 2005. The long-standing installations have proven to be economically advantageous while being extremely environmentally friendly.

02 · Fuel security

Fuelwood Plantation & Processing

EnerFab’s dedicated fuelwood plantation at Nawagattegama.

EnerFab is a pioneering company that provides energy solutions in biomass energy using gasification technology. We have completed many industrial energy conversions in different industries. As an energy partner, EnerFab operates in every part of the business, including fuelwood supply and fuelwood plantations, to give customers a safer fuelwood supply.

The EnerFab fuelwood plantation was established at Nawagattegama in the Puttalam district. This model plantation has 40,000 plants at a spacing of 2 by 1 metres. It was established in the Maha season of 2008, and the plants have now grown to maturity. Before the plantation, the land was scrubland. It is now lush green vegetation that enriches the soil with nitrogen, supplies fuel to industry and improves the microclimate.

Why you need a reliable fuelwood supplier

If you are investing in a power plant, you should have considerable control over your own fuelwood supply. This is a requirement when you plan the project and seek approvals from government authorities.

If your supply networks fall short, the entire project fails unless your own supply carries it until the network works properly. In our opinion, your own share should be at least 50%, although different people suggest different percentages.

Why you should select EnerFab as your fuelwood supplier

  • As a fuelwood supplier, we have a market that can be linked with plantations.
  • We have a supplier base of more than 2,000 all around the island, other than the North and East.
  • We have collected different species of fuelwood, such as Gliricidia, Ipil Ipil, cinnamon, timber off-cuts, coconut shells and other approved fuelwood trees.
  • We know the comparative advantages of each fuel type, where it is available and its seasonality.
  • We have developed a transport system for the fuelwood business and can scale operating capacity up or down at any time.
