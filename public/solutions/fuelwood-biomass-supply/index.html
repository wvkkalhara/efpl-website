Sustainable fuelwood and biomass supply

A secure fuel supply for your biomass plant: our own energy plantation, thousands of suppliers and machines we designed to handle the wood.

Energy partner, end to end

Your fuel, secured

EnerFab pioneered gasification-based biomass energy in Sri Lanka and has completed industrial energy conversions in many sectors. As your energy partner, we cover every part of the business, including fuelwood supply and plantations, to keep your plant safely supplied.

Fuelwood comes from short-rotation coppicing of fast-growing leguminous trees, collected through a network of 3,000 farmers and a supplier base of more than 2,000 across the island, outside the North and East.

The land was scrubland before planting. It is now lush green vegetation that enriches the soil with nitrogen, supplies fuel to industry and improves the local microclimate.

Why a power plant needs a reliable fuelwood supplier

If you invest in a biomass power plant, you need real control over your own fuelwood supply. Authorities expect it when you plan the project and seek approvals.

If supply networks fall short, the whole project fails unless your own supply carries it until the network recovers. In EnerFab’s view, your own share should be at least 50%.

Why choose EnerFab as your supplier

  • A market that can be linked directly with plantations
  • More than 2,000 suppliers across Sri Lanka, outside the North and East
  • Gliricidia, Ipil Ipil, cinnamon, timber off-cuts, coconut shells and other approved fuelwood
  • We know each fuel’s advantages, where it grows and its seasonality
  • Our own transport system, scalable up or down at any time
