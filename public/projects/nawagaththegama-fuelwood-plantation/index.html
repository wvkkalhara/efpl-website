The project

EnerFab established a model fuelwood plantation at Nawagaththegama in the Puttalam district. It has 40,000 plants at a spacing of 2 by 1 metres, planted in the Maha season of 2008, and the plants have now grown to maturity.

Before planting, the land was scrubland. It is now green vegetation that enriches the soil with nitrogen, supplies fuel to industry and improves the local microclimate.

The plantation shows how EnerFab’s gasifiers can run on wood that is grown, cut and regrown, alongside the network of farmers who supply EnerFab with fuelwood.

Energy Plantation

How an energy plantation works

Fast-growing leguminous trees such as Gliricidia are cut back regularly and regrow from the stump, a method called short-rotation coppicing. The trees fix nitrogen, so the land improves while it produces fuel.
