01 · Fuelwood

Fuel from sustainable energy plantations

Gliricidia, Ipil Ipil, cinnamon, timber off-cuts and coconut shells, collected through a network of 3,000 farmers and grown on our own 40,000-plant plantation at Nawagaththegama.

02 · Hopper

Chopped to size, fed from above

Fuelwood sticks are cut into small pieces for the gasifier. Our DM500 wood chopper handles 500 kg an hour, and our EWG 1 wood grab lifts a cubic metre of chips at a time.

03 · Reactor

Solid wood becomes a clean-burning gas

Gasification converts solid fuel into a combustible gas called producer gas. EnerFab designs and fabricates its gasifiers in Sri Lanka.

04 · Heat

Heat for boilers, kilns and dryers

Producer gas is piped just like LPG to a burner. With a dual-fuel burner you keep your boiler, and your old fuel stays on as backup. One rubber dryer has run this way since 2006, replacing 50 litres of diesel an hour.

05 · Power

Electricity from waste wood

Producer gas runs an internal combustion engine and generator. At a wood processing plant, an EnerFab gasifier generator makes 200 kW from the factory’s own waste wood.

06 · Since 2005

Running for more than a decade

Kandalama Hotel, Tea Factory Hotel, CW Mackie and other establishments have operated EnerFab gasifiers for over ten years, with EnerFab after-sales service throughout.

Furnace oil and diesel to biomass

Keep your boiler. Change the fuel.

A gasifier with a dual-fuel burner switches a diesel or furnace-oil boiler to producer gas. You keep operating the same boiler, and the fuel you used before stays available as a backup.

Compared with a wood-fired boiler, this conversion gives better efficiency, a better response to changes in load, and lower particulate emissions. It works for furnaces, kilns and dryers too.

Biomass gasifiers and CHP

Nawagaththegama, Puttalam district

From scrubland to an energy forest

Our model fuelwood plantation was planted in the Maha season of 2008: 40,000 plants at 2 by 1 metre spacing, on land that was scrubland. Today it is lush, mature growth that adds nitrogen to the soil, supplies fuel to industry and improves the microclimate.
