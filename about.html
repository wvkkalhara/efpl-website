Re-greening Sri Lanka, one industry at a time.

Since 2005 EnerFab has helped Sri Lankan industry replace imported fossil fuel with locally grown, renewable biomass.

Who we are

Biomass technology, plus the fuel to run it.

EnerFab was founded in 2005 with the vision to provide biomass-based renewable energy solutions to local industry.

Since then, EnerFab has been offering turn-key biomass technological solutions along with biomass supplied from sustainable energy plantations. The biomass is extracted through a collection network of 3,000 farmers.

Our team of qualified and experienced civil and mechanical engineers also delivers steel fabrication, mini-hydro, material-handling and energy-efficiency projects.

Mission

What we set out to do.

  • To promote the growing of trees, shrubs, plantations and living organisms as a natural and renewable source of food, fertility and fuel.
  • To promote the construction and installation of equipment for processing Sri Lanka’s natural and renewable resources for the environmentally conducive generation of electrical and thermal energy through solar, hydro, biomass and wind.
  • To design, fabricate, install and operate structures and equipment towards achieving the above.

Milestones

Continuously serving industry since 2005.

EnerFab-installed gasifiers have been in operation for over a decade in several industries in Sri Lanka.

  1. 2005

    EnerFab founded

    Established to provide biomass-based renewable energy solutions to local industry.

  2. 2006

    First diesel-to-biomass dryer

    A dryer’s diesel burner was replaced by a producer-gas burner and gasifier. The unit has been in operation from 2006 to date.

  3. 2008

    Nawagattegama plantation

    40,000 fuelwood plants established in the Maha season on former scrubland in the Puttalam district.

  4. 2009

    GUGLER hydro partnership

    In December, Aitken Spence Power signs for GUGLER hydro turbines for one of its mini-hydro sites. EnerFab is assigned the machinery installation and local technical support.

  5. 2010

    4.5 MW Kirkoswald project

    In March, Bogo Power awards GUGLER a turnkey contract for three Francis turbines.

  6. 2017

    200 kW electrical system

    Proved that producer gas is clean enough to run an internal combustion engine.

  7. 2018

    Poultry farm LPG replacement

    In July, EnerFab undertook to install a gasification plant to replace LPG at a poultry farm in Puttalam. The project is still ongoing.
