ESCO

The project

EnerFab’s ESCO division has completed more than 15 detailed energy audits in knitted-garment factories, tea factories and other sectors.

An audit measures where a factory’s energy goes and where it is wasted. EnerFab then implements the energy-saving measures on a turnkey basis, with a payback guarantee.

What an energy audit covers

A preliminary audit is a walk-through that finds the obvious savings. A detailed audit uses metering instruments to measure steam, compressed air, electricity and heat use, then costs each saving and its payback period.
