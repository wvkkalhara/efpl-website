The project

The dryer at a rubber processing plant burned 50 litres of diesel every hour. EnerFab replaced the diesel burner with a producer-gas burner fed by an EnerFab biomass gasifier.

The conversion was made in 2006, and the dryer has operated on producer gas from then to date.

The video below shows the installation.

Gasifier installed by EnerFab to replace 50 litres per hour of diesel in a rubber processing plant

How a diesel dryer switches to biomass

The dryer itself stays. Only the burner changes: the diesel burner is replaced with one designed for producer gas, and a gasifier beside the dryer makes the gas from wood. The flame heats the dryer air just as the diesel flame did.

The same approach works for boilers, furnaces and kilns running on diesel, furnace oil or LPG. Read converting from furnace oil to biomass.
