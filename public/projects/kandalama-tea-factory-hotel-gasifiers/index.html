Hotels

The project

Two of Sri Lanka’s best-known hotels in the Aitken Spence group, Kandalama Hotel and the Tea Factory Hotel, run EnerFab biomass gasifiers. Both installations have operated for more than a decade, and EnerFab has provided after-sales service throughout.

The Tea Factory Hotel installation was covered by the Daily News in November 2007.

Hotels need steady heat every day of the year. A gasifier lets them make that heat from locally grown biomass instead of imported fuel, with EnerFab supplying spare parts and breakdown maintenance to keep the plant running.

How a gasifier serves a hotel

A gasifier heats wood with a limited supply of air, so instead of burning it turns the wood into a combustible gas called producer gas. The gas is piped to a burner on the existing heating equipment, where it burns with a clean, controllable flame.

Because the fuel is wood, the hotel’s heating no longer depends on the price of diesel or furnace oil. Read how biomass gasification works for the full process.
