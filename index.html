Who we are

Green engineering, from plantation to power.

EnerFab was founded in 2005 with the vision to provide biomass-based renewable energy solutions to local industry through biomass gasifiers and CHP systems.

Since then, EnerFab has been offering turn-key biomass gasifier solutions along with biomass supplied from sustainable energy plantations. The biomass is extracted through a collection network of 3,000 farmers and through our own fuelwood plantations.

Biomass energy solutions

Keep your boiler. Change its fuel.

We offer biomass gasifiers for industrial heating and electrical power generation.

Biomass gasifiers with a dual-fuel burner are offered to switch diesel or furnace-oil-fired boilers to producer gas from the gasifier. This lets you keep operating the same boiler on producer gas, while the fuel you used before stays available as a backup.

Unlike wood-fired boilers, our conversion procedure gives better efficiency, a better response to load variation and fewer particulate emissions.

Biomass in

Fuelwood such as Gliricidia, chopped to size, is fed into the gasifier.

Gasification

Gasification converts the solid fuel into a combustible producer gas.

Piped like LPG

The gas is piped to a burner on your boiler, dryer or kiln, or into a gas engine.

Heat & power

Clean thermal energy, electricity, or both as combined heat and power.

Fuelwood plantations

From scrubland to lush green energy crop.

The EnerFab fuelwood plantation was established at Nawagattegama in the Puttalam district. This model plantation has 40,000 plants at a spacing of 2 by 1 metres. It was established in the Maha season of 2008, and the plants have now grown to maturity.

Before the plantation, the land was scrubland. It is now lush green vegetation that enriches the soil with nitrogen, supplies fuel to industry and improves the microclimate.

EnerFab is also linked with fuelwood suppliers to meet the demand for biomass from sustainable energy crops.
