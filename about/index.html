Re-greening Sri Lanka since 2005

A Sri Lankan engineering company replacing imported fossil fuel with locally grown, renewable biomass.

Who we are

Technology, plus the fuel to run it

EnerFab was founded in 2005 with a vision to provide biomass-based renewable energy solutions to local industry.

Since then, EnerFab has delivered turnkey biomass technology together with biomass from sustainable energy plantations, collected through a network of 3,000 farmers. Our qualified civil and mechanical engineers also deliver steel fabrication, mini-hydro, material handling and energy-efficiency projects.

Vision
  • Re-greening Sri Lanka
  • Restoring Sri Lanka’s pristine environment
  • Revitalising rural employment and prosperity throughout Sri Lanka
  • Re-appreciating Sri Lanka’s natural resources
Mission
  • To promote growing trees, shrubs, plantations and living organisms as a natural, renewable source of food, fertility and fuel.
  • To promote building and installing equipment that processes Sri Lanka’s natural, renewable resources to generate electricity and heat in an environmentally sound way, through solar, hydro, biomass and wind.
  • To design, fabricate, install and operate the structures and equipment to achieve this.

Milestones

EnerFab-installed gasifiers have operated for over a decade in several Sri Lankan industries.

  1. 2005

    EnerFab founded

    Set up to bring biomass renewable energy to local industry.

  2. 2006

    Diesel dryer converted

    A rubber processing dryer moved from a diesel burner to producer gas, and has operated from 2006 to date.

  3. 2007

    Named the leading gasifier maker

    A University of Moratuwa review in Tech Monitor named EnerFab the leading designer and manufacturer of gasifiers in Sri Lanka.

  4. 2008

    Nawagaththegama plantation

    40,000 fuelwood plants planted on former scrubland in the Puttalam district.

  5. 2009

    GUGLER hydro installation

    EnerFab installed GUGLER turbines and provided technical support at an Aitken Spence Power mini-hydro site.

  6. 2017

    200 kW electrical system

    A gasifier generator running on waste wood proved producer gas can run an internal combustion engine.

  7. 2018

    Poultry farm, Puttalam

    EnerFab undertook a gasification plant to replace LPG at a poultry farm.
