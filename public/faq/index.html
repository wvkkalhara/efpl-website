Technical questions, answered

What industrial buyers ask us most about gasifiers, fuel supply and our engineering divisions.

Biomass gasifiers

What can an EnerFab biomass gasifier replace?

Diesel, furnace oil (HFO) and LPG fired boilers, furnaces, kilns and dryers, and the fuel in internal combustion engines that drive electrical generators.

Do I need to replace my boiler to switch to biomass?

No. A biomass gasifier with a dual-fuel burner switches your diesel or furnace-oil boiler to producer gas. You keep operating the same boiler, and your previous fuel stays available as a backup.

Why use a gasifier instead of a wood-fired boiler?

EnerFab’s conversion method gives better efficiency, a better response to load variation and lower particulate emissions than a wood-fired boiler.

Can a gasifier generate electricity?

Yes. Producer gas can run an internal combustion engine that turns a generator. EnerFab installed a gasifier generator at a wood processing plant that makes 200 kW from waste wood, with a further 400 kW of thermal energy available from the engine’s exhaust and cooling system. EnerFab also builds combined heat and power plants.

What sizes and efficiencies are EnerFab gasifiers?

A University of Moratuwa review in Tech Monitor (2007) reported EnerFab gasifiers for electricity from 1 to 250 kWe with about 30% overall efficiency, and thermal gasifiers in Sri Lanka from 100 to 350 kW with claimed efficiencies of 70 to 80%. Each system is sized to your load, so contact us for current figures.

How long do EnerFab gasifiers last?

Kandalama Hotel, Tea Factory Hotel, CW Mackie and other establishments have operated EnerFab gasifiers for more than a decade. A rubber processing dryer converted from diesel has operated from 2006 to date.

Who operates and maintains the plant?

EnerFab offers management contracts to operate the plants it supplies, and provides spare parts and breakdown maintenance for every installation it has completed. We have provided after-sales service since 2005.

Fuelwood and biomass supply

What fuelwood can be used?

Gliricidia, Ipil Ipil, cinnamon, timber off-cuts, coconut shells and other approved fuelwood trees. A wood processing plant can run a gasifier on its own waste wood.

Can EnerFab supply biomass on contract?

Yes. EnerFab supplies biomass on a contractual basis from its energy plantations and an out-grower network: a collection network of 3,000 farmers and a supplier base of more than 2,000 across Sri Lanka, outside the North and East.

How much of a power plant’s fuel should it control itself?

In EnerFab’s view, at least 50%. If outside supply networks fall short, your own supply has to carry the plant until they recover.

What wood-handling equipment does EnerFab make?

The EWG 1 wood grab, which grabs 1 m³ of chips at 20% moisture (US$ 10,000), and the DM500 wood chopper, which processes 500 kg of fuelwood an hour (US$ 5,000).

Steel, hydro, cranes and ESCO

What steel structures does EnerFab fabricate?

Steel lattice structures, steel buildings, large advertising structures, steel penstock pipelines, water, diesel and HFO storage tanks and pipelines, small hydro power plants and other civil works, designed to international standards with corrosion protection.

Which hydro turbines does EnerFab supply?

EnerFab is the sole agent in Sri Lanka for GUGLER Water Turbines: Kaplan, Pelton and Francis turbines from 5 kW to 10 MW, horizontal or vertical, with design, installation and servicing.

What crane capacities are available?

Single girder overhead travelling and suspension cranes to 12.5 tons over 30 m, double girder overhead travelling cranes to 160 tons over 30 m, gantry and semi-gantry cranes to 160 tons over 50 m, jib cranes to 5 tons and light cranes to 2 tons.

What do EnerFab’s ESCO services include?

Preliminary and detailed energy audits, other energy studies, and turnkey energy-saving projects with a payback guarantee. EnerFab has completed more than 15 detailed energy audits.
